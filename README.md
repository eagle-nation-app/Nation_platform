1. Introduction

NATION is a social connection platform built around a simple idea: people should have a better way to find others who share their interests, ambitions, goals, and preferences.

Today, the internet makes it easy to communicate with people from almost anywhere in the world. However, finding people with whom we genuinely share common interests or ambitions is not always easy. Many people have ideas they want to develop, goals they want to achieve, and knowledge they want to share, but they may not know where to find the right people.

This is the problem that inspired me to develop NATION.

The goal of NATION is to make it easier for people to discover compatible individuals, build meaningful connections, exchange ideas, and explore opportunities to work together.

I would also like to express my sincere appreciation to my mentor for the guidance, support, and knowledge shared throughout this project. The experience has helped me learn more about software development and turn an idea into a practical project.

2. The Problem

Although social media platforms connect millions of people, having access to a large number of users does not necessarily mean finding the right connections.

Some common challenges include:

- Difficulty finding compatible people: It can be challenging to discover individuals who share similar interests, goals, or ambitions.
- Too much unrelated content: Users may spend time browsing through people and content that do not match what they are looking for.
- Limited opportunities for collaboration: Students, developers, researchers, and innovators may struggle to find people interested in working on similar ideas.
- Connections without shared purpose: Some online interactions do not develop into meaningful relationships or productive collaborations.

I wanted to explore a different approach: instead of focusing only on connecting people, what if a platform could help them discover people with whom they have something meaningful in common?

3. The Solution: NATION

NATION is designed to help users discover and connect with people based on shared interests, ambitions, goals, and preferences.

The platform focuses on compatibility-based discovery and mutual interest. Rather than allowing one person to establish a connection without the other person's agreement, the intended connection process gives both individuals the opportunity to express interest.

When two people show mutual interest, they can establish a connection and communicate.

Through this approach, NATION aims to support:

- Meaningful social connections.
- Academic discussions and learning.
- Research and project collaboration.
- Professional networking.
- Sharing ideas and opportunities.
- Building communities around common interests.

My vision is to make NATION a place where a connection can become the beginning of an idea, a project, a collaboration, or a lasting relationship.

4. Main Features

The following are the main features and capabilities planned for NATION:

4.1 User Profiles

Users can create profiles that introduce who they are, what interests them, and what they hope to achieve.

A profile can help other users understand someone's interests, ambitions, goals, and preferences.

4.2 Interest and Preference Selection

Users can indicate the interests and preferences that matter to them. This information can help the platform identify people with similar interests.

4.3 Compatibility-Based Discovery

NATION is intended to help users discover people based on similarities between their profiles and preferences.

The objective is to make discovering relevant people more useful than browsing through random profiles.

4.4 Mutual Connections

A connection is intended to be established when both users express interest in connecting.

This approach respects the choices of both individuals.

4.5 Messaging

After a mutual connection is established, users can communicate, exchange ideas, and discuss possible collaborations.

4.6 Collaboration Opportunities

NATION aims to support people who want to work together on academic, research, professional, creative, or technology-related projects.

5. How NATION Works

The intended user journey follows these steps:

Step 1: Create an account

A new user registers to access the platform.

Step 2: Build a profile

The user adds relevant information about themselves, including their interests, goals, and ambitions.

Step 3: Select preferences

The user identifies the types of people and interests they would like to discover.

Step 4: Discover people

NATION helps users explore individuals who may have similar interests or compatible preferences.

Step 5: Express interest

A user can express interest in connecting with another person.

Step 6: Establish a mutual connection

When both individuals express interest, a connection can be established.

Step 7: Communicate and collaborate

Connected users can communicate, exchange ideas, and explore opportunities to work together.

6. How to Use the Platform

The general process for using NATION is:

1. Open the NATION application.
2. Register or sign in, depending on account availability.
3. Complete your profile.
4. Add your interests, ambitions, and preferences.
5. Explore the people suggested by the platform.
6. Express interest in people you would like to connect with.
7. Connect when the interest is mutual.
8. Use the available communication features to exchange ideas.

The availability of individual features depends on the current development and implementation stage of the application.

7. Who Can Benefit from NATION?

NATION is intended for people who want to build connections around shared interests and goals.

Some potential users include:

- Students: Find people with similar academic interests, exchange knowledge, and study together.
- Developers: Discover other programmers and explore software development projects.
- Researchers: Find people interested in similar research areas and discuss ideas.
- Entrepreneurs: Connect with individuals who share business interests and ambitions.
- Innovators: Discover people who want to develop new ideas and solve problems.
- Professionals: Expand their networks and explore opportunities for collaboration.

NATION is not limited to one academic field, profession, or community. Its broader goal is to make meaningful connections more accessible.

9. Running the Project Locally

To run the project on a local computer, follow these steps.

Requirements

- A computer
- Visual Studio Code
- Node.js
- npm
- A Gemini API key, if the application requires it

Step 1: Clone the repository

Open the terminal and run:

git clone YOUR_GITHUB_REPOSITORY_URL

Step 2: Open the project in VS Code

cd NATION
code .

Step 3: Install dependencies

npm install

Step 4: Configure environment variables

Create a ".env.local" file in the project's root directory if it does not already exist.

Add your API key:

GEMINI_API_KEY=YOUR_GEMINI_API_KEY

Replace the placeholder with your actual key. Do not share or upload the key publicly.

Step 5: Start the development server

npm run dev

Step 6: Open the application

After starting the development server, the terminal will display a local address. Open that address in your web browser to access the application.
